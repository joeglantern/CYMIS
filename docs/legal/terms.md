# Terms of use

Version 0.1, effective [effective date]

> **Draft.** For your organisation's legal team to review before use. Words in square brackets are placeholders to fill in. Until it is approved, this page is guidance, not a binding document.

## Who these terms are for

Cymis is a container audit tool run by [organisation name] ("your organisation", "we"). These terms apply to everyone who signs in to it: yard crew, supervisors and admins. By signing in you agree to them.

Cymis is an internal work tool. It is not open to the public and it is only for audit work your organisation has given you.

## Your account is yours alone

- Your staff ID and password are personal. Do not share them, write them on the phone, or sign in for someone else.
- Everything done under your account is recorded against your name, including every scan, review and change.
- If you think someone knows your password, change it straight away and tell your supervisor.
- If you leave your job or change roles, your account is closed or changed by an admin. Do not keep using it.

## Using the phone and the app

- Only scan containers that are part of an audit you were given.
- Take photos of container doors and numbers only. Avoid people, faces, vehicle number plates and anything unrelated to the audit. See the photo guidance page.
- Do not change, delete or hide scans to make a count look better. Report problems to your supervisor instead.
- Keep the phone locked when you are not using it. Cymis locks itself after 10 minutes in the background.
- Do not copy, export or share audit data outside your organisation unless your manager has approved it.

## What is not allowed

- Trying to get into accounts, data or parts of the system you were not given.
- Testing the system for weaknesses without written permission from your organisation.
- Using automated tools to send scans or read data, other than the official integrations an admin sets up.
- Uploading anything other than photos taken for an audit.

## Suspension

An admin can pause or close an account at any time to protect the audit, the data or other staff, for example after repeated wrong passwords, a suspected leak, or misuse. Misuse may also be handled under your organisation's staff rules.

## Availability and limits

We work to keep Cymis running and your scans safe, including when you have no signal. Even so, the service is provided as it is, and there may be downtime for maintenance or problems outside our control. To the extent the law allows, your organisation is not liable for losses caused by downtime or by decisions made only on Cymis data without the usual checks.

Audit results are a record of what was scanned. They support, and do not replace, your organisation's own procedures for releasing, moving or investigating containers.

## Changes to these terms

If these terms change, the new version is shown in the app with a new version number and date. Important changes are announced by your supervisor or in the app before they apply.

## Questions

Ask your supervisor, or contact [system owner name and email].
