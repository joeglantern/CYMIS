# Photo guidance

Version 0.1, effective [effective date]

> **Draft.** For your organisation's legal team to review before use. Words in square brackets are placeholders to fill in. Until it is approved, this page is guidance, not a binding document.

Every scan saves one photo of the container door. It proves the box was there and lets the server check the number and colour a second time. A good photo is about the box, nothing else.

## Do

- Fill the frame with the door and the painted number.
- Stand square to the door, not at a sharp angle.
- Use the torch at night or in deep shade.
- Wipe the camera lens when it gets dusty.

## Avoid

- People and faces. Wait a moment or move so they are out of shot.
- Vehicle number plates and truck cabs.
- Paperwork, screens, or anything with personal details on it.
- Photos for anything other than the audit.

> If someone ends up in a photo by accident, tell your supervisor. They can mark the scan so the photo is reviewed.
