# Privacy notice

Version 0.1, effective [effective date]

> **Draft.** For your organisation's legal team to review before use. Words in square brackets are placeholders to fill in. Until it is approved, this page is guidance, not a binding document.

## Who is responsible

[Organisation name], [address], is the data controller for personal data in Cymis. Questions about your data go to our data protection officer: [name], [email], [phone].

This notice follows the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and its regulations.

## What we collect

| Data | Where it comes from | Why we need it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full name, staff ID and role | Your admin, when your account is made | To sign you in and show who did each scan |
| Password | You | To sign you in. Stored only as a one way hash, never readable |
| Scans: container number, time, method | The app, when you scan | The audit itself |
| Photo of the container door | The phone camera, when you scan | Proof the box was seen, and a second check of the number and colour |
| Location at the moment of a scan | The phone, if you allow location | To show where each box was found on the yard map |
| Device id and device name | The app | To tell phones apart and to sign out a lost phone |
| Sign in times, IP address, actions taken | The server | Security, and a record of who changed what |

> Cymis does not track your location all day. It only reads it at the moment you save a scan, while the scanner is open.

Container photos are meant to show the door and the number. If a person or a vehicle number plate appears by accident, that image is still personal data and is handled under this notice.

## Our lawful basis

- **Legitimate interests and contract:** keeping an accurate record of containers in the yard is part of your work and our operations.
- **Legal obligation:** some records may be kept because customs, port or tax rules require them.
- **Consent:** for location on your own phone, where your organisation does not issue the device. You can turn location off in the phone settings; scans still save without it.

## Who can see it

- Crew see their own audits and scans.
- Supervisors see the audits they run, including scans, photos and locations.
- Admins manage accounts and can see the activity log.
- Systems your organisation connects through official integrations can read audit results, never passwords.
- Our hosting provider stores the data for us under a written agreement and may not use it for anything else.

We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

## Where it is stored

Data is stored on servers at [hosting provider and country]. Photos are kept in private storage that is only reachable through short lived links. If data is stored outside Kenya, we do so only with the safeguards the Act requires, and we will tell you where.

## How long we keep it

See the data retention schedule for each kind of data and the reason for its period.

## Your rights

Under the Act you have the right to:

- be told how your data is used, which is what this notice is for
- get a copy of your personal data
- ask us to correct data that is wrong
- ask us to delete data we no longer have a reason to keep
- object to processing based on legitimate interests
- withdraw consent where we rely on it, without affecting what was done before

Some audit records cannot be deleted while they are needed for legal or operational reasons. If we cannot do what you ask, we will tell you why. We reply within the time the Act allows.

To use any of these rights, contact the data protection officer at [email].

## Complaints

If you are not happy with how we handled your data, tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) at www.odpc.go.ke.

## Changes

When this notice changes, the new version appears in the app with a new version number and date.
