# Legal pages

> **Draft.** For your organisation's legal team to review before use. Words in square brackets are placeholders to fill in. Until it is approved, this page is guidance, not a binding document.

These are shown in the app under Help, Legal, and at `/legal/<page>` on the web dashboard.

- [Terms of use](terms.md): The rules for using Cymis at work.
- [Privacy notice](privacy.md): What personal data Cymis keeps, why, and your rights under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019.
- [Data retention schedule](retention.md): How long each kind of data is kept, and why.
- [Security and incident policy](security.md): How we protect the data, and what happens if something goes wrong.
- [Photo guidance](photos.md): How to take scan photos that do their job and respect people's privacy.
- [Third party licences](licences.md): Open source software and fonts Cymis is built with.
