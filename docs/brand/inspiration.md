## What we take

1. **Scanner:** camera dimmed, white brackets, one yellow scan line. On a hit the brackets snap tight and a half sheet rises with the number and the result, camera still live behind it, with a "left in this block" counter.
2. **Yard map as a dashboard view:** each block as a grid of bay cells coloured by bucket, with a thin progress bar per block. Uses our status colours from `colours.md` (found green, missing red, unexpected yellow, needs review royal), not the colours in the references.
3. **One big number per card**, Apple style, everything else quiet.
4. **Review queue:** a faint tint on rows that need review, and a floating black bulk action bar when rows are selected.
5. **Clay assets** in one serious matte tone, no faces, one KPA colour accent each, only in empty, offline and done states.
6. **Two splashes:** the Check Digit film on first open of the day, and a quick CSS or Rive "containers stacking" version on every other launch, under 1.2 seconds.
